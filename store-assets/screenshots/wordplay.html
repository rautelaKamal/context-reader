Now is the winter of our discontent

Plain meaningWhat’s the point?Line by lineHow it works

Richard establishes the play's triumphant mood and his own cynical opportunism by mapping a political regime change directly onto the seasonal cycle through weather metaphors.

winter of our discontent

This phrase uses the cold, barren season as a direct synonym for the misery of the previous Lancastrian rule, preparing the reader for a metaphorical temperature shift.

glorious summer

By pairing this phrase with the previous "winter", the text frames the new Yorkist victory not just as a political event, but as a complete biological and emotional thaw.
