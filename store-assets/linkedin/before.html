Explaining Swiftbefore the fix

“A young healthy child well nursed is at a year old a most delicious, nourishing, and wholesome food.” Jonathan Swift, A Modest Proposal (1729)

Plain meaning What’s the point? Line by line How it works

The writer cites an anonymous authority to propose, with chilling matter-of-factness, that human infants can be used as a delicious food source.

Their position

Human babies make excellent, nutritious food when they reach one year of age.

Tone

Monstrously clinical and detached; the writer presents the idea with the calm expertise of a cookbook author.

A reader who had never met the essay would believe Swift meant it.
