“A young healthy child well nursed is at a year old a most delicious, nourishing, and wholesome food.” Jonathan Swift, A Modest Proposal (1729)

Plain meaning What’s the point? Line by line How it works

The writer proposes, with chilling calm, that one-year-old babies should be eaten — a satire, not a suggestion.

Their position

The writer does not literally believe that children should be eaten; this is a savage satirical proposal designed to shock the reader into recognizing the extreme neglect and starvation suffered by the Irish poor.

Tone

Satirical and monstrously detached; the horror is masked by clinical, culinary language.
