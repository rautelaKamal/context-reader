=== NAME ===
ContextReader — explain a poem, an editorial, a paper

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (132 max) ===
Select a poem, an editorial or a dense paragraph and see what the writer actually means — read in context, on the page.

=== DETAILED DESCRIPTION ===
Some sentences stop you even though you know every word in them.

A line of Shakespeare. A newspaper editorial whose argument you cannot quite
pin down. A paragraph in a paper outside your field. A poem that clearly means
more than it says.

ContextReader is for those. Select the passage and it tells you what the writer
is actually saying.

READS THE PARAGRAPH, NOT JUST YOUR SELECTION

Most tools define words. That is rarely the problem. You cannot say what a line
of verse means from the line alone, and you cannot explain what an editorial is
arguing against without the sentences that set it up. ContextReader sends the
surrounding paragraph too, so it answers the question you actually have.

FIVE WAYS TO READ A PASSAGE

• Line by line — verse worked through one line at a time, each line quoted as
  it stands. Poetry, Shakespeare, song lyrics, scripture.
• What's the point? — the writer's position, what they are arguing against,
  and their tone. Catches irony and sarcasm, which are easy to read straight.
• How it works — word choice, sound, where the line breaks, and words doing two
  jobs at once. It found the pun on "sun of York" in Richard III unprompted.
• Plain meaning — the same thing said in ordinary English.
• The jargon — technical terms defined in the sense this text uses them.

It offers only the lenses that suit the passage and picks one for you. Switch
with a single tap.

GO DEEPER

Answers arrive in about two seconds. When that is not enough, Go deeper hands
the same passage to a slower, more careful model — the one that notices a word
held at a line break so it reads two ways.

IT DOES NOT INTERRUPT

Selecting text shows a small pill, and nothing happens until you ask. No popup
fires while you are highlighting a line to read it.

GOOD FOR

Students reading set texts and papers. Anyone working through a daily
editorial. Readers of poetry. People reading in English above their comfort
level. Anyone who has re-read the same sentence four times.

FREE, WITH A DAILY ALLOWANCE

30 explanations a day, resetting at midnight; a deep read uses three. The
allowance exists because the service runs on a shared free quota — it keeps the
extension working for everyone rather than letting one long session exhaust it.

PRIVACY

The passage you select and the page address are sent to the explanation service
and passed to Google's Gemini API, which produces the explanation. Nothing is
stored on our servers, no account is required, and no selected text is written
to any database or log. Google may use data sent to its free API tier to
improve its products; see the privacy policy for detail.

Open source: github.com/rautelaKamal/context-reader

=== SINGLE PURPOSE ===
Explains passages the user selects while reading a web page.

=== PERMISSION JUSTIFICATION ===

Host permission on all sites: the extension is a reading aid, and people read
on arbitrary websites. It cannot know in advance which pages a reader will need
help with. The content script only reacts to a deliberate selection followed by
a click or keyboard shortcut; it does not read, collect or transmit page
content otherwise.

Storage: records the count of explanations used today, so the daily allowance
can reset at midnight. No personal data is stored.

=== DATA DISCLOSURE ===
Collected: Website content (the selected passage and the page address).
Not sold to third parties. Not used or transferred for any purpose unrelated
to the item's single purpose. Not used to determine creditworthiness or for
lending purposes.
