=== NAME ===
ContextReader — understand what the writer means

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (132 char max) ===
Select a passage that lost you. Get what the writer actually means, read in context, without leaving the page.

=== DETAILED DESCRIPTION ===
Some sentences stop you even though you know every word in them.

An editorial whose argument you cannot quite pin down. A line of Shakespeare.
A paragraph in a paper outside your field. A poem that clearly means something
more than it says.

ContextReader is for those. Select the passage and it tells you what the writer
is actually saying — read together with the paragraph around it, not as an
isolated string of words.

WHY CONTEXT MATTERS

Most tools define words. That is rarely the problem. You cannot say what a line
of verse means from the line alone, and you cannot explain what an editorial is
arguing against without the sentences that set it up. ContextReader sends the
surrounding paragraph too, so it can answer the question you actually have.

FIVE WAYS TO READ A PASSAGE

• Plain meaning — the same thing said in ordinary English
• What's the point? — the writer's position, what they are arguing against,
  and their tone, including irony that is easy to miss
• Line by line — verse worked through one line at a time
• How it works — word choice, sound, line breaks, deliberate double meanings
• The jargon — technical terms defined in the sense this text uses them

It picks the one that suits the passage and offers only the lenses that make
sense for it. You can switch with a single tap.

GO DEEPER

Answers arrive in about two seconds. When that is not enough, Go deeper hands
the same passage to a slower, more careful model — the one that notices a word
held at a line break to make it read two ways.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

It does not interrupt. Selecting text shows a small pill, and nothing happens
until you ask. No popup fires while you are highlighting to read.

FREE, WITH A DAILY ALLOWANCE

30 explanations a day, resetting at midnight. A deep read uses three. The
allowance exists because the service runs on a shared free quota — the limit
keeps it working for everyone rather than letting one long session exhaust it.

PRIVACY

The passage you select and the page address are sent to the explanation
service and passed to Google's Gemini API, which produces the explanation.
Nothing is stored on our servers, no account is required, and no selected text
is written to any database or log. Note that Google may use data sent to its
free API tier to improve its products; see the privacy policy for detail.

=== PERMISSION JUSTIFICATION ===

Host permission on all sites: the extension is a reading aid, and people read
on arbitrary websites. It cannot know in advance which pages a reader will
need help with. The content script only reacts to a deliberate selection
followed by a click or keyboard shortcut; it does not read, collect or
transmit page content otherwise.

Storage: records the count of explanations used today, so the daily allowance
can reset at midnight. No personal data is stored.
