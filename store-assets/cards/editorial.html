Their position

The writer argues that the central government ("the Centre") has a false understanding of its relationship with regional states, treating cooperation ("federal comity") as "a favour it bestows" when it is actually a legal "obligation the Constitution imposes."

Responding to

Protests from southern states regarding the latest Finance Commission award, as mentioned in the preceding text, where states argue "that they are penalised for having controlled their populations."

Tone

Admonitory and firm, carried by the corrective phrase "disabuse itself of the notion" and the contrast between a voluntary "favour" and a mandatory "obligation".
